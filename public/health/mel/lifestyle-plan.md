## Part 1 — Exercise

### Two Phases

**Phase 1 — Now (while chest tension and elevated T3 persist)**
Walking and strength training only. No moderate-to-high intensity cardio. This isn't a limitation — resistance training is actually the most effective single intervention for fasting glucose, and walking delivers real anti-inflammatory benefit. Both are safe right now.

**Phase 2 — Once T3 normalizes and chest tension resolves**
Add Zone 2 cardio: sustained effort at a pace where you can speak in full sentences but wouldn't want to sing. Stationary bike, swimming, or brisk walking all count.

### Strength Training — Full Body (3x/Week)

**Format:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps per exercise | 60–90 sec rest between sets
Use a weight where the last 2 reps of each set are genuinely hard but your form stays clean.

**Warm-up (5 min):** March in place → arm circles → hip rotations → 10 bodyweight squats

| Exercise | Muscles Targeted | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | Quads, glutes, core | Dumbbell or kettlebell |
| Romanian deadlift | Hamstrings, glutes, lower back | Dumbbells or barbell |
| Dumbbell bench press or push-ups | Chest, shoulders, triceps | Dumbbells or bodyweight |
| Bent-over dumbbell row | Back, biceps | Dumbbells |
| Dumbbell overhead press | Shoulders | Dumbbells — start light |
| Plank hold | Core | Bodyweight — 30–45 sec hold |
| Glute bridge | Glutes, lower back | Bodyweight or barbell across hips |

**Cool-down (5 min):** Hip flexor stretch → hamstring stretch → chest opener → slow deep breathing

**Progression:** Start at 60–70% of a challenging weight. Add 5 lbs to lower body movements and 2.5 lbs to upper body movements every 2 weeks as they feel manageable.
